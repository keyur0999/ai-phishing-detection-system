Core Capabilities

Designed to help users quickly evaluate suspicious links and communication.

URL & Link Inspection

Evaluates web destinations against typosquatting patterns, misleading subdomains, suspicious TLDs, and known malicious databases.

Email Text Analysis

Analyzes messages for artificial urgency, credential-harvesting triggers, and automatically extracts and checks any embedded links.

QR Code Verification

Decodes QR codes via camera or image upload so you can review where the code actually leads before loading the page on your device.

How It Works

A simple three-step process to verify suspicious communication.

1. Paste or Scan

Provide a URL, message content, or QR code that you want to check for potential deception.

2. Multi-Layer Analysis

The system evaluates structural heuristics, runs local classifier inference, and queries VirusTotal threat feeds.

3. Clear Findings

Get a direct verdict (Safe, Suspicious, or Phishing), a risk score from 0 to 100, and a concise list of detected anomalies.
