How Threat Analysis Works

PhishGuard uses a three-layer pipeline to identify dangerous destinations without sending sensitive data to third-party language models.

1. Heuristic & Structural Rules

Checks domain age, typosquatting variants (e.g. paypa1 vs paypal), raw IP addresses, suspicious top-level domains (.xyz, .top), and deceptive URL paths.

2. Local Machine Learning

Evaluates text tokens and lexical distributions using lightweight local scikit-learn models (TF-IDF and character n-grams) running entirely on-device.

3. VirusTotal Threat Intelligence

Cross-references targets with VirusTotal's v3 multi-vendor database to verify findings against 70+ global cybersecurity antivirus and blocklist feeds.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about scanning, privacy, and detection accuracy.

Is my email content kept private?

Yes. Text analysis runs offline via local machine learning models on the server. Your message text is never sent to external AI providers or public APIs.

How does VirusTotal verification work?

When you scan a URL, PhishGuard checks whether any of 70+ global security vendors have cataloged the domain or link as malicious, phishing, or malware.

Can I scan QR codes from my phone?

Yes. The QR scanner supports live camera detection on laptops and smartphones, as well as uploading QR image files directly from your photo library.
